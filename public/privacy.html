Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Data Controller

ANERIUM OnePass is the data controller for personal data processed through this platform. Data is stored on servers located in Nuremberg, Germany (EU).

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email, full name, phone, avatar, date of birth
  • Business data: business name, location, categories, discounts, campaigns
  • Transaction data: loyalty transactions, membership plans, subscriptions
  • Support data: support tickets, chat messages, CRM records
  • Authentication data: password hash, OAuth tokens (Google), 2FA/OTP secrets

3. Legal Basis

  • Contract: Account creation and service delivery (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Consent: Marketing communications (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — withdrawable at any time
  • Legitimate interest: Security logging, fraud prevention (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

4. Data Storage

All data is stored on servers located in the European Union (Germany). No data is transferred to third countries. Backups are encrypted and stored locally with WAL archiving for point-in-time recovery.

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained while account is active
  • Backups: 30-day retention (local), WAL archives for PITR
  • Security logs: 14-day retention with log rotation

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Right to access (Art. 15)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16)
  • Right to erasure / right to be forgotten (Art. 17)
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20)
  • Right to object (Art. 21)
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3))

To exercise these rights, contact: support@anerium.com

7. Security Measures

  • SSL/TLS encryption (Let's Encrypt) for all connections
  • JWT authentication with rate limiting
  • Security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, COEP, COOP, CORP)
  • SSH key-only access (password auth disabled)
  • CORS locked to anerium.com and anerium.de
  • Database with WAL archiving and daily encrypted backups

8. Marketing Consent

Marketing communications require explicit opt-in. You can manage your preferences in account settings or contact support@anerium.com to withdraw consent.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth: used for optional sign-in (Google's privacy policy applies)
  • OpenAI: used for AI features (OpenAI's privacy policy applies)

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests: support@anerium.com
